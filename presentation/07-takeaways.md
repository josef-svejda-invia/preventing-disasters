# 7. Takeaways

- Disasters are **chains of small mistakes**, not single dramatic ones — and
  every one of them passed review.
- The fixes aren't fancy. They come from one habit:

> **You will make mistakes. So will your colleagues. So will the AI.**
> **So: catch them in more than one place — and when you catch one, be LOUD.**

## About that AI

AI learned from the **average** of all the code humans have written — and the
average is full of exactly these bugs. It will happily hand you a swallowed
exception and a fail-open default, because millions of us did.

AI won't save you from Therac-class thinking, because **AI _is_ the average — and
the average is what killed those patients.** The judgment that sits above the
average is the part that's yours. That's the part worth learning. That's the part
that isn't going anywhere.

## One ask

Next time you're about to type `// this can't happen` — **write a test, or make
it loud, instead.** Cover the mistake before someone makes it.
